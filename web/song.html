Round the earth by night and day,
MODIS and VIIRS are scanning,
Gathering up the signs of fires.

Gliding the sky by satellite.
A photo path but with some gaps,
At the equator - missing insight.

VIIRS, with pixels extra sharp
Does her daily scannings.
With a clearer view at night,
For seeing fire warnings.

Combine the data. Get more clues.
Study history's records.
That's how we predict the threats,
and take appropriate measures.
