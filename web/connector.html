Ask your own AI about fire

This is a connector: it lets the AI chat app you already use ask a 26-year satellite fire record, for any of 235 countries and territories, in plain words.

The record comes from NASA's two fire cameras in space, the older MODIS (since 2000) and the newer VIIRS (since 2012), put on one scale, so you can ask "is this place burning more than usual?" and get a straight answer.

The address to give your AI app

This address is for AI apps, not for a web browser: opening it here shows only a short error, which is normal.

How to add it

  1. Add it as a custom connector (MCP server) in your app's settings, and paste the address above. Nothing to install, no account, no key.
  2. Ask your AI a question about fire in a place, like the ones below.

Tried so far: a command-line AI (OpenAI's Codex) answered a question with it on November 15, calling three of its tools. Other apps have not been tried yet; any app that takes a custom MCP connector should work the same way.

Three questions to try

"How bad were the fires in Australia in December 2019?"

"Australia's December 2019 was far busier than normal, with about three times the usual amount of fire. That's about 13,000 fire detections in December 2019."

"Is Argentina burning right now?"

"On the 26-year record, Argentina's early November is about normal, with almost exactly the usual amount of fire." It also counts what NASA's satellites saw in the last 24 hours.

"Will Argentina have a bad fire season next year?"

"Nothing about next season is certain, but here are the patterns to watch. Argentina's fire season usually runs from July to September, and it's usually highest in August."

Real answers the connector gave on November 14. Your AI may word them its own way; the numbers come from the connector.

What it can do: eleven tools

  • fire_now Is it burning right now? NASA's last 24 hours, with the record's answer for the same place.
  • fire_answer The answer for a place and a period: a month, a year, the latest month or the last fire season.
  • place_record Every year since 2000 for one place, or one year month by month.
  • how_sure How far the joined record holds up in that country.
  • report_sentence One sentence for a report or an article.
  • find_places Search the 235 countries and territories.
  • compare Two places, or two periods of one place.
  • viirs_scale A year or month on the newer VIIRS camera's scale.
  • nasa_recent Straight from NASA: the last 24 hours, 48 hours or 7 days for a country or a map box.
  • nasa_archive Straight from NASA, for chosen dates; needs your own free NASA map key, and says how to get one.
  • nasa_sources What NASA data it reaches, and NASA's own caution.

What the numbers are

Hot spot detections, not fires or land burned. A detection is one 5 km patch of ground where a satellite saw fire on one day, so one big fire counts many times.

The record, never a forecast. Ask about the future and you get the patterns to watch, not a prediction. For what to do on the ground, follow local authorities and emergency services.
